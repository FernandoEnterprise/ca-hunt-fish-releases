CA Hunt & Fish Guide — Support

Found wrong or outdated regulation data?

That's the most important thing you can report. Please open an issue with the water/zone name and what you saw — every value in the app carries a "verified as of" date and a link to its official source, which helps us fix things fast. In the meantime, always rely on the official source linked from that screen.

App problems, questions, feature requests

GitHub issues is the fastest channel for those too.

Subscriptions & billing

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To manage or cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

A reminder

CA Hunt & Fish Guide is an independent, unofficial reference — not affiliated with CDFW or the State of California. Regulations change; always verify with official CDFW sources before you go.
