Privacy Policy

CA Hunt & Fish Guide — effective August 28, 2026

The short version: we collect nothing.

CA Hunt & Fish Guide does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Details

  • Your settings stay on your device. Your residency selection, interests, favorites, and your acceptance of the Terms of Use are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • Location. If you allow location access, it is used only to show your position on the regulations map, on your device. Your location is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Dataset updates. The app periodically downloads its regulation dataset from a static file server (GitHub Pages). These are ordinary anonymous file downloads; we do not receive or store information about who downloads them.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple account. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • External links. The app links to official government websites (such as wildlife.ca.gov) and lake operators' official pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on our public data repository: github.com/FernandoEnterprise/ca-hunt-fish-releases.

CA Hunt & Fish Guide is an independent, unofficial reference and is not affiliated with the California Department of Fish and Wildlife or the State of California.
